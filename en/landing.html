How to study

Read in short sessions and do the practice in the suggested time. Progress stays in this browser. Make copies using the journey’s export option.

Lesson images are illustrations generated in Codex, not video results. To finish production, you need access to a compatible video generator and editor. Check credits and features first.

The 24-hour challenge

The deadline helps limit the project. It does not guarantee access, immediate generation, or professional quality. If there is a queue, access block, or pending review, continue in another session. Public posting is optional; you can deliver a file or private link.

Who it is for

It is for you if you

  • You want to make your first product or local-business video.
  • You use phone photos and have little time per session.
  • Prefer to learn by doing.

It is not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want technical material.
  • Want a traditional video lesson.
